Since 2023

A club built to make failing cheap.

ZUMS Computer Society is the student technology community of ZNRF University of Management Sciences. We exist so that a first-year who has never opened a terminal can get to a working project without paying for the privilege of being a beginner.

What we do

Six wings run their own weekly rhythm — a contest, a build night, a lab rota, a critique session. On top of that sit the things the whole club shows up for: the hackathon, the CTF night, the orientation, and the semester showcase where every wing has to demo something that runs.

Everything is deliberately low-ceremony. There is no audition. There is a form, a twenty-taka semester fee, and a WhatsApp group you get added to once the panel has matched your payment.

How we are run

An executive committee is elected each academic year and hands over in writing. The treasurer keeps a single ledger, and period totals from that ledger are published on the transparency page — no member names, no receipts, just where the money went.

Certificates are issued against attendance, not registration, and every issued certificate carries a code that anyone — including an employer — can check on the verification page.

The motto is not decoration

TRY · FAIL · REPEAT

It is printed on the logo because it describes the method. Sessions are built so the first attempt is expected to break. Projects are picked so a failed one still teaches. Handover notes are published so next year's panel fails at new things instead of the same ones.

History

How we got here

  • 2023

    Eleven people and a whiteboard

    The club is founded with two wings — programming and hardware — and no budget at all.

  • 2024

    Six wings, first HackFest

    The design wing produces the brand you are looking at. HackFest runs for the first time, for 90 people.

  • 2025

    Certificates and a real ledger

    Attendance-based certificates go live. The treasurer moves the accounts off a spreadsheet.

  • 2026

    312 members, 13 partners

    The member portal replaces the paper forms, and the transparency page goes public.
