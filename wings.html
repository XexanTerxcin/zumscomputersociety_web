Six wings

Where the work actually happens

A wing is a standing group with its own lead, its own weekly slot and its own budget line. Pick a primary wing when you join so somebody is responsible for you — then attend anything else you like. Most members end up in two.

Not sure yet?

You do not have to decide today

Join the club first and mark any wing as your primary. Wing leads run an open session in the first two weeks of every semester, and switching afterwards is one message to the general secretary — no form, no fee.
