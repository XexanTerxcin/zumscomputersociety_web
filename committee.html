Governance

Executive committee

Elected each academic year and handed over in writing. Past panels stay listed — continuity is the point, and last year's work should still have its name on it.

Standing for election

How the panel is chosen

  • Any member with two semesters of paid membership may stand.
  • Nominations open in August; the vote is at the annual general meeting.
  • Officers serve one academic year and may stand once for re-election.
  • The outgoing panel publishes handover notes and a closing ledger position before leaving.
