Terms of membership

The rules that come with a ZCS membership, written to be read rather than skipped.

1. Who may join

Membership is open to any currently enrolled student of ZNRF University of Management Sciences, from any department. Membership ends automatically when you cease to be enrolled, at which point you may move to the alumni directory.

2. Fee

The membership fee is BDT 20 per academic semester and is not refundable once your membership has been approved. Event fees are separate and are refundable up to 72 hours before the event starts.

3. Conduct

  • Treat other members, guests and staff with respect. Harassment of any kind ends a membership.
  • Club equipment is shared. Log what you borrow, return it in the state you found it, and report damage rather than hiding it.
  • Security work is done with permission and within the law. Testing a system you do not own or have written authorisation for is grounds for removal and may be reported.
  • Do not use the club name, logo or partner relationships for anything the committee has not approved.

4. Your work

Anything you build at a club event or in a club project remains yours. We ask only for permission to show it on the projects page and in club material, with your name on it. Where a project is jointly built, credit is shared with everybody who worked on it.

5. Certificates

Certificates are issued against verified attendance, not against registration. A certificate obtained by misrepresentation will be revoked, and revocation is visible on the public verification page.

6. Photography

We photograph events and publish albums. Tell any organiser if you do not wish to be photographed, and ask us to remove any picture you appear in — we will, without argument.

7. Site use

  • Accounts are personal. Do not share credentials.
  • Do not attempt to disrupt, overload or gain unauthorised access to this site. Report anything you find to the panel instead — responsible disclosure is welcome and credited.
  • Content on this site belongs to the club or to the members who wrote it. Reuse with attribution for non-commercial purposes is fine.

8. Changes

These terms may change with the constitution. Material changes are announced on the announcements page at least fourteen days before they take effect.

9. Questions

Anything unclear goes to the panel via the contact form. The constitution itself is in the document library.
