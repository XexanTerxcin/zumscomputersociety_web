Who we are

          
A computer society that runs on failure, on purpose.

          

            We started in 2023 with a whiteboard and eleven people. Today the club runs six wings,
            an open lab rota, a partner network of thirteen organisations, and a certificate
            programme that an employer can verify from a code on the page.
          

          

            Nothing here requires you to already be good at it. The competitive programming ladder
            starts at problem one. The robotics bench keeps spare parts for exactly this reason.
