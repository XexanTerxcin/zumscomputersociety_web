Privacy policy

Short version: we collect what running a membership needs, we keep it while you are a member, and we never sell or share it.

What we collect

  • At registration: full name, student ID, program, academic session, email address, WhatsApp number, a formal photograph and your chosen wing.
  • For the fee: the payment method, the transaction ID or the name of the panel member who took cash, and the period the payment covers.
  • While you are a member: event registrations, attendance check-ins, certificates issued to you, points awarded, and any reimbursement claim you file.
  • Technical: a session cookie, and request logs kept for abuse control.

Why we collect it

To run your membership: to confirm you are an enrolled student, to match your fee against a payment, to put your name on a certificate, and to reach you when an event changes. Nothing is collected for advertising, and there is no third-party analytics on this site.

What is public

Your public profile shows your name, ZCS ID, position, wing, program, skills, points and certificates. It never shows your email address, phone number, student ID or payment details. You can ask for your profile to be hidden from the directory at any time.

Who can see the rest

  • The executive committee, for the parts of it their role needs.
  • The treasurer, for fee and reimbursement records only.
  • Nobody outside the club, ever. We do not sell, rent or share member data.

How long we keep it

Membership records are kept while you are a member and for two years afterwards, so that a certificate can still be verified and an alumni record can be maintained. Payment records are kept for seven years as part of the club ledger. Request logs are kept for thirty days.

Your choices

  • See everything we hold about you from your dashboard.
  • Correct anything wrong yourself, on the profile page.
  • Ask for your photo to be removed from any album you appear in.
  • Ask for deletion. We will remove everything except the ledger rows we are required to keep, which are reduced to an amount and a date.

Security

Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Uploads are validated by inspecting the file's own bytes rather than trusting its extension. Forms carry a CSRF token, public forms are rate limited, and sign-in is locked out per address and per account.

Contact

Any question about this policy goes to the panel, or through the contact form. We answer within two working days.
